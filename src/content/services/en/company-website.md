I build company websites that load fast, that your team can edit, and that Google reads correctly.

## What goes wrong with most company sites

- Slow on phones, so people leave before they see anything
- Every text change needs the web developer
- Good-looking, but unclear about what the company does or how to reach it
- Invisible on Google, because no page matches what customers search for

## What I build

- **Fast.** Pages are built ahead of time, not assembled on every visit.
- **Editable.** The team adds articles and changes text itself.
- **One page per service.** Someone searching for that service lands on a page that answers them.
- **Easy to contact.** The LINE button is visible before the first scroll.
- **Two languages.** Thai first, English when you need it.

## Steps

1. Talk through who your customers are and what they search for
2. Plan the pages and content
3. Design and build, shown to you in rounds
4. Set up Google Search Console and hand over
5. Train the team to edit content
