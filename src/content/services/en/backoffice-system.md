I build back-office systems for businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets and chat groups. I start by watching how your team works, then build the system to match.

## When spreadsheets stop being enough

Most businesses start with spreadsheets and a chat group, and that works while the team is small. The trouble starts as the work grows.

- Files exist in several versions, and nobody knows which is current
- Approvals get buried in chat history
- One person on leave stops a whole line of work
- Nobody can tell who changed a figure, or when
- Month-end reports mean merging files by hand

If any of these sound familiar, a back-office system helps.

## What a back-office system does

It is the software the team uses for daily work: taking orders, requesting approval, scheduling, keeping customer records. Everyone sees the same data.

- **One place for the data.** No more asking which file is the latest.
- **Permissions by role.** Staff see their own work; managers see the whole picture.
- **A history of every change.** You know who did what, and when.
- **Reports ready to read.** No merging files at month end.

## How I work

1. **A 30-minute call.** Tell me the problem. No charge.
2. **See the real work.** I watch how the team works and write down the scope.
3. **A one-page quote.** Scope, timeline and revision rounds.
4. **Built in rounds.** You try the real thing after every round.
5. **Handover and training,** then support after handover.

## Start small

I recommend starting with the one task that hurts most, such as purchase requests or staff transport. Once the team is comfortable, add the next module. You see results sooner and the whole company does not change its way of working at once.
