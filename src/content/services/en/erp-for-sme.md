I build ERP systems for SMEs that contain only the parts your business uses. Start with the one module that hurts most, then add more.

## Big-vendor ERP is often too big

Off-the-shelf ERP is designed to fit every business, so it carries parts you use and parts you never will.

- You change your process to fit the program
- The team learns screens more complex than the work
- Costs rise with every user
- Your own special steps usually cost extra to customise

## An ERP that fits

A custom ERP connects the work of several departments, following the steps the team already uses.

- Sales opens an order; the warehouse sees it at once
- Purchasing requests approval; the manager approves in the system, not in a chat
- Management sees the whole picture in one report

## Modules I build

| Module | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Sales and customers | Customer records, quotes, orders |
| Purchasing and approval | Purchase requests, multi-step approval |
| Stock | Goods in, goods out, balances |
| People and transport | Schedules, staff transport |
| Reports | A cross-department view for management |

These are examples. I build to your real work, not a fixed package.

## Start with one module

Changing the whole company at once is risky. I start with the module where the problem is worst, let the team get comfortable, then add the next module on the same data.

If your work stays in one department, a single [back-office system](/en/services/backoffice-system/) may be enough.
